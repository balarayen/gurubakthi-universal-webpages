Lord Murugan's most beloved protective hymns (kavasam), in a calm, easy-to-read Tamil library — on your iPhone and iPad.

What's inside

  • Sri Kandhar Sashti Kavasam — the revered kavasam of Sri Devaraya Swamigal.
