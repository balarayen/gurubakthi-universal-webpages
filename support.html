Frequently asked questions

How do I continue reading where I left off?
The app remembers your place automatically. When you reopen a book, it resumes at your last reading position.
Does the app work without internet?
Yes. All texts are included in the app, so you can read fully offline — no connection needed.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 16 or later.
Does the app collect my data?
No. Guru Bakthi collects no personal data and uses no tracking or ads. Your reading position is stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
I found a mistake in a text, or have a text to suggest.
Please email us the details — corrections and suggestions are very welcome.
The app isn't behaving as expected.
Try closing and reopening the app. If the problem continues, email us your device model and iOS version and we'll help.
